Privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026

What AFTERFOLIO keeps

The photos you choose for a job, how you framed them, the job details, your business profile, and the videos and PDF reports you create. All of it stays in the app’s storage on this device.

What we collect

Nothing. AFTERFOLIO has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it does not send your photos, videos or reports to any server.

Your photo library

AFTERFOLIO opens your library only when you choose a photo or a logo, and adds a video to it only when you tap Save to Photos.

Sharing

A video or report leaves this device only when you share it, through the system share sheet, to the app you pick.

Deleting

Deleting a job removes its photos, videos and reports from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Files you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them.

Children

AFTERFOLIO is a tool for service businesses and is not directed to children.
