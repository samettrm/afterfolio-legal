AFTERFOLIO

Privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026

What AFTERFOLIO keeps

The photos you choose for a job, how you framed them, the job details, your business profile, and the videos and PDF reports you create. All of it stays in the app’s storage on this device.

What we collect

AFTERFOLIO never uploads your photos, videos or reports. It sends only the small amount of data described below, so the app, its paid plan and its ads work.

Usage events

Which features are used (for example that an export finished), with a random identifier for this installation, the app version, the device type and the country our server derives from the connection. No name, e-mail address, photo or text you write is included, and the connection address is not stored.

Optional account

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we keep the provider’s account identifier and, for Google, a one-way fingerprint of your verified e-mail address. Nothing requires an account. You can delete it in the app, under Business.

Purchases

AFTERFOLIO Pro is paid through the App Store or Google Play. We receive the store’s signed record of the subscription (product, dates, price, refunds) to confirm it; we never see your payment details.

Ads

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. On iPhone and iPad the ads are not personalized and AFTERFOLIO does not ask to track you. On Android, Google may use the advertising ID according to the consent choices you make. Google describes its use of that data in its own privacy policy. AFTERFOLIO Pro shows no ads.

Your photo library

AFTERFOLIO opens your library only when you choose a photo or a logo, and adds a video to it only when you tap Save to Photos.

Sharing

A video or report leaves this device only when you share it, through the system share sheet, to the app you pick.

Deleting

Deleting a job removes its photos, videos and reports from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Files you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them.

Children

AFTERFOLIO is a tool for service businesses and is not directed to children.
